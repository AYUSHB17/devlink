Away from this network

Screens

Use one mouse and keyboard across both machines: move the cursor off the edge that faces the other device and it continues there. Drag files to that edge to send them.

Arrangement

Get your mouse back at any time with Ctrl + Option/Alt + Shift + Esc, or just move the other machine's own mouse.

Clipboard

Anything you copy is sent, encrypted, to every connected paired device. Turn this off before copying secrets, or use “Push clipboard” on a device for one-off sends.

Or drag a link (or a URL from the address bar) onto the shared screen edge, or press Cmd+Option+Shift+L in your browser to send the current tab.

Port forwarding

Shortcuts
